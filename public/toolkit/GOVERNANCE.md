# Governance v0.1 — initial policy

The project is public infrastructure. Maintenance authority follows sustained, reliable contribution, not founder ownership, financial sponsorship or institutional prestige.

## Founder / Steward

The founder initially coordinates direction, draft methodology and community formation. No person is named in this seed repository. The initial steward must be identified publicly before exercising repository governance. The steward may later become an advisor. They have no permanent veto and no permanent entitlement to be lead maintainer.

While fewer than three active maintainers exist, the recorded steward can administer routine changes after public review, documenting decisions. Core methodology remains proposed until an RFC is accepted. No one may describe provisional review as independent validation. Bootstrap decisions remain reviewable by the subsequent maintainer group.

## Domain maintainers

Domains include Marine Engineering, Energy / Pyrolysis, Remote Sensing, Cost Model, Data, Web and Translation. All positions are initially unassigned. A candidate provides a public history of sustained correct contributions, review quality, reproducibility and appropriate domain competence. An existing maintainer sponsors a nomination, followed by 14 days of public discussion. Two-thirds of eligible active maintainers approve appointments, with at least three eligible participants once the group has formed. Before that, the steward records provisional appointments and their evidence.

An active maintainer has contributed, reviewed or recorded maintenance availability within 90 days. Planned absences are recorded without penalty. For unexplained inactivity, contact the maintainer, allow 30 days to respond, then record an inactive role. Reinstatement follows the same public process. Review authority is domain-specific; no unqualified reviewer can validate maritime safety merely by maintaining web code.

## Lead Maintainer and succession

The lead coordinates releases, cross-domain review and access handover. Any active maintainer can nominate themselves or a consenting candidate with contribution evidence. After 14 days of discussion, appointment requires two-thirds of non-conflicted active maintainers (minimum three eligible participants once established). The lead is reviewed annually and may be replaced with the same process. The founder’s vote has the same weight as another eligible maintainer’s vote.

Record repository-owner access, domain control, hosting, signing and financial responsibilities in `docs/governance/maintainers.md`; never record secret values. Maintain at least two accountable access holders when available. A departing lead transfers control and documents outstanding work. If the founder stops daily work, maintainers continue under these rules.

## Decisions and RFCs

Routine corrections use PR review. A public RFC is mandatory for changes to LCOCR definitions, evidence levels, challenge evaluation, core data boundaries, governance, licenses or major technical-route standards.

RFCs state motivation, boundary changes, evidence, alternatives, compatibility, translation effects and failure conditions. Minimum discussion is 14 days. Seek consensus; unresolved substantive choices use a two-thirds vote of eligible active maintainers. Record voters, conflicts, dissent, decision date and reasons. Materially revised proposals reopen discussion. License changes also require the rights needed from applicable copyright holders; a vote cannot relicense third-party work.

## Conflicts, appeals and emergencies

Disclose financial, employer and authorship conflicts. Authors do not independently verify their own evidence; conflicted maintainers recuse from decisive review. Anyone can appeal with a public documented rationale; an uninvolved maintainer leads reconsideration. Contentious unresolved science remains uncertain rather than being decided true by vote.

For credible urgent security or safety problems, a maintainer may temporarily remove a dangerous link or restrict a vulnerable endpoint. Preserve records privately where necessary, explain the action as soon as safe, and review within seven days. Emergency action cannot silently rewrite the cost metric, remove dissent or change licensing.
