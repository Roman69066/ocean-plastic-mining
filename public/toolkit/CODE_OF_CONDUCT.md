# Code of conduct

Critique claims, methods and code. Treat people with respect across language, nationality, background and discipline. Clear disagreement, failed experiments and good-faith corrections are welcome.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, impersonation, fabricated evidence and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish personal information without permission. Do not pressure researchers to suppress negative results or reveal unrelated confidential work.

Once repository administrators are identified, report incidents through a private repository contact or GitHub’s reporting controls. No confidential email address is invented in V1. If no private channel exists, do not post identifying allegations publicly; use the platform’s abuse report mechanism.

An uninvolved maintainer reviews a report with confidentiality and due process, documents the factual basis, and applies proportionate action: request correction, warning, temporary restriction, or removal for severe or repeated conduct. Recuse when conflicted. Appeals go to an uninvolved maintainer or future lead. Enforcement records disclose only the minimum necessary information.
