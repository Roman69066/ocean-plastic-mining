# RFC-0001: Initial LCOCR and evidence boundaries

Status: PROPOSED
Created: 2026-09-12
Author: initial project specification (no appointed maintainer)
Decision: pending

## Proposal

Adopt `docs/methodology/lcocr.md` and `verification.md` as version 0.1 after public review. Gross LCOCR includes annualized capital and mutually exclusive full annual costs divided by verified dry tonnes permanently removed. Revenue is reported separately.

Retain both T1 operating balance and T1 full-cost balance because the initial brief uses both boundaries. The homepage uses the conservative full-cost threshold; neither becomes proven through publication. T2 requires resource revenue to cover full cost. T3 is distant research.

Separate E0–E6 quality from supported/uncertain/rejected direction. Draft leaderboard admission requires E5 or E6 plus independent audit and complete boundary records; E6 requires actual independent replication, not only audit.

## Open questions

- What final-fate documentation and time horizon suffice for permanent removal?
- What conditions and duration justify “sustained” operation for a particular system?
- How should uncertainty, salvage, decommissioning, correlated downtime and ecosystem impacts be compared?
- What prevents low €/t from masking unacceptable bycatch or emissions?

## Alternatives

Net-cost ranking can obscure reliance on subsidy or uncertain resource sales. Capture-only tonnes can hide losses. A universal fixed trial duration can reward unrepresentative weather. These alternatives need evidence before adoption.

## Migration and dissent

Any acceptance or revision must version formulas, schemas, translation explanations and prior records. No votes, approvals or independent validators are recorded yet. Methodology is not frozen.
