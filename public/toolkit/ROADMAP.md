# Roadmap — reduce unknowns

Every milestone requires inspectable evidence, not a calendar promise. All milestones are pending.

| Stage | Unknown to reduce | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| R0 — Methodology frozen | Can reviewers agree on cost and removal boundaries? | RFC-0001 reviewed and accepted, versioned schema, dissent recorded. Current state: draft, not frozen. |
| R1 — Energy / logistics baseline | What physical lower bounds remain? | Reproducible energy balance and logistics ledger with sensitivity and references. |
| R2 — LCOCR baseline | Which full-cost inputs dominate? | Complete ledger, dry mass boundary and independent recalculation; unknowns explicit. |
| R3 — First verified component test | Does one component work under its declared conditions? | Raw observations, calibration, uncertainty, failures and independent review. |
| R4 — First field prototype | Does performance survive actual marine conditions? | Approved test plan, environmental monitoring, recovery plan and full trial data. |
| R5 — T1 feasibility | Can revenue plus public payment sustain teams? | Both operating and full-cost balances, verified service/payment assumptions and sensitivity. |
| R6 — Multi-team replication | Can independent teams reproduce results? | Independent operational datasets and comparable E6 evidence where justified. |

T2 is an unproven longer-term economic threshold. T3 and long-term offshore residence remain distant research, not deployment promises.
