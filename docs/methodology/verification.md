# Evidence and verification v0.1-draft

Conclusion direction: **SUPPORTED**, **UNCERTAIN**, **REJECTED**. Evidence quality: **E0–E6**. These are independent dimensions.

| Level | Meaning | Minimum inspectable basis |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | Hypothesis | Falsifiable claim, scope and assumptions. |
| E1 | Back-of-envelope | Units, arithmetic, rough inputs and limitations. |
| E2 | Analytical model | Reproducible model, inputs, allocations and sensitivity. |
| E3 | Laboratory evidence | Raw data, calibration, protocol and lab boundary. |
| E4 | Controlled field test | Field conditions, controls, uncertainty and documented outcomes. |
| E5 | Operational evidence | Sustained real-ocean operation, complete ledger, failures and traceable removal. |
| E6 | Independent replication | A genuinely independent team reproduces relevant operational results under a comparable boundary. |

No calendar duration alone guarantees E5. Reviewers must justify whether the test duration and sea conditions represent the claimed operation. A lab test cannot be promoted to offshore evidence by confidence or endorsement.

Draft leaderboard eligibility: E5 or E6, full cost ledger, annualization, location, duration, dry tonnes, final-fate chain, uncertainty, source rights and independent audit of the submission. E5 audit does not make it E6 replication. Separate comparable conditions and evidence tiers. No fictional entrants. `data/leaderboard/schema.json` defines the structure for future records; admission remains a human evidence decision.

If evidence conflicts, retain the records, show **Conflicting evidence** and leave the claim uncertain pending resolution. Publish null/negative outcomes, revisions and rejected claims with an audit trail. A core assumption may be visibly rejected; no such event is invented in the current seed data.
